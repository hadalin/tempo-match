Privacy

Tempo Match has no accounts, sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics. Your autoplay setting stays in your browser.

Searching

Your searches are not logged. Songs are gathered ahead of time from GetSongBPM, Deezer, Spotify and ReccoBeats, and nothing about you is ever sent to them.

If a song won't play, part of your network address is kept for up to 24 hours to spot songs that many people report, then thrown away.

Spotify player

The player comes straight from Spotify, so Spotify sees the usual things any website sees and may set its own cookies. See the Spotify Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/hadalin/tempo-match/issues.
